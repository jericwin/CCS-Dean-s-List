How it works

From grades to recognition in four steps.

Every application moves through the same transparent path, and you get notified at each step.

01

Apply online

Fill in your course, term and GWA, then upload your Certificate of Grades and Certificate of Enrollment.

02

SBO screening

SBO officers verify your documents and GWA, and leave remarks if anything needs fixing.

03

Dean's review

Endorsed applications are ranked and forwarded to the Dean for final approval.

04

Get recognized

Approved students join the official Dean's List for the term. You'll see it first in your notifications.
